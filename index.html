Privacy Policy

At MoneyStation: Expense Tracker (AKA MoneyStation), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and financial data.

1. Overview

MoneyStation is a local-first expense/income tracking app. We do not require user accounts, do not operate servers that store your data, and do not sell or share user information.

2. Personal Information Collection & Use

A. Information You Provide:
MoneyStation does not collect or transmit personal information such as names, email addresses, bank credentials, or payment card numbers.
Financial information that you manually enter (such as amounts, categories, and dates) is stored locally on your device to provide the app's functionality. This information never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

B. Notification Data (Sensitive Permission):
The App requires Notification Access permission to detect payment notifications from supported payment and banking applications and other notifications that contain payment-related information.

  • Purpose: To read transaction-related notification content and help automate expense/income entries.
  • Scope: The App is designed to process supported payment app notifications and other notifications that match payment-related keywords. It is not intended to collect personal draft or unrelated content.
  • Processing: Notification content is processed locally on the device. A limited recent notification history may be stored locally on the device to support expense/income detection, duplicate prevention, and user-initiated diagnostics.
  • Sharing: Notification data is not sent to our servers and is not shared with third parties.

C. Learned Preferences & Automation:
MoneyStation may store learned preferences locally on your device, such as merchant-to-category mappings and optional automatic expense/income handling rules based on your prior confirmations. These preferences are used only to improve the app's on-device expense detection and categorization experience.

D. Device Permissions:

  • Notification Access: Required to detect and parse payment notifications from supported payment applications.
  • Storage/Files: Required for manual or scheduled data exports or local backups to internal storage.

3. Data Sharing

MoneyStation does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties. No analytics services, advertising SDKs, or external tracking systems are integrated into the App.

4. Data Security & Deletion

All user expense/income data is stored locally on the device using the device's secure storage system. No external servers are used to store your financial information or expense/income records.

You have the absolute right to delete your data at any time via the "Reset app" function or by uninstalling the App.

5. Third-Party Services & License Verification

MoneyStation fetches its live exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API via their free public endpoint: https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/${base}. The app doesn't use any other third-party services or license verification.

MoneyStation does not send your expense/income history, notification content, bank account information, transaction details, or locally stored budget data to any external services or entities. Your financial records remain stored locally on your device.
